 Real-Time Competition Dashboard

During the competition, every player and the host should see a live dashboard that updates instantly.

Display:

- Total Players Joined
- Players Currently Online
- Players Finished
- Players Still Answering
- Players Disconnected
- Competition Status (Waiting, Starting, Live, Finished)

Example:

Players Joined: 48
Online: 46
Finished: 12
Still Answering: 34

------------------------------------

# Player Information

When a player joins, display:

- Profile Picture (optional)
- Display Name
- Current Learning Level
  - Beginner
  - Intermediate
  - Advanced
  - Expert
- Current Rank
- Total XP
- Country (optional)

Example:

🟢 Victor
Level: Intermediate
XP: 1,250
Rank: #18

------------------------------------

# Live Join Notifications

Whenever someone joins, instantly display a notification to everyone in the room.

Examples:

🟢 Victor joined the competition.
🟢 Grace joined the competition.
🟢 John joined the competition.

The player count should update immediately without refreshing the page.

------------------------------------

# Live Progress Tracker

During the quiz, show:

Players Joined: 50
Finished: 15
Still Answering: 35

Update these numbers in real time after every submission.

------------------------------------

# Live Leaderboard

After every question, automatically update the leaderboard without refreshing.

Display:

🥇 1st - Victor (1,250 pts)
🥈 2nd - Grace (1,210 pts)
🥉 3rd - David (1,180 pts)

Also display:

- Current Level
- XP
- Accuracy Percentage
- Correct Answers
- Wrong Answers
- Average Answer Speed

------------------------------------

# Experience (XP) System

Award XP after every competition.

Correct Answer: +10 XP
Fast Answer Bonus: +5 XP
Winning a Competition: +100 XP
Perfect Score: +50 XP

XP should increase the player's level automatically.

Levels:

Level 1–10: Beginner
Level 11–25: Intermediate
Level 26–50: Advanced
Level 51+: Expert

Show the player's level badge beside their name throughout the competition.
